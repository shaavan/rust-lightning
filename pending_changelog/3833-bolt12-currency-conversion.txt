# API Updates

 * BOLT 12 offers may now use ISO 4217 currency-denominated amounts. Offer
   creators are responsible for only advertising currencies they can price when
   responding to invoice requests. Payers and invoice responders need a
   `CurrencyConversion` implementation when a currency amount must be converted
   to millisatoshis.

 * `ChannelManager::new` and `ChannelManagerReadArgs` now take a
   `currency_conversion` argument. Users who do not support currency conversion
   may use `NullCurrencyConversion`.

 * BOLT 12 invoice request and invoice handling now use currency conversion when
   resolving payable amounts for currency-denominated offers. Unsupported
   currencies or invalid converted amounts fail when an invoice request or
   invoice amount is verified.

 * `InvoiceRequest::amount_msats()` now returns only the explicitly encoded
   invoice request amount. Use `InvoiceRequest::payable_amount_msats()` to
   obtain the payable amount, including amounts inferred from the offer and
   quantity. `InvoiceRequest::has_amount_msats()` has been removed.

 * `Bolt12PaymentError` now includes `UnverifiableAmount` and
   `ExcessiveAmount`. `Bolt12SemanticError` now includes `ExcessiveAmount`.
   Users matching these enums exhaustively must handle the new variants.

# Backwards Compatibility

 * Downgrading to LDK 0.2 while a currency-denominated BOLT 12 payment is
   pending will prevent `ChannelManager` deserialization. Ensure that no such
   payments are pending before persisting `ChannelManager` state for downgrade.
